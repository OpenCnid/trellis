The spine · the one hypothesis the whole study turns on

Turner: “A principle is something you commit to in advance so you can't talk yourself out of it later.”  Trellis: “Prose is not a constraint; it is a wish.”

Two houses, one claim — a commitment with no structural enforcement is not binding; it is talked away the moment an incentive pushes. The study's job is to test that resonance against the text, with Anthropic (refused, and won in court) standing ready as the positive control — not to assume it.

The named doctrines do the reading. Custody answers where a commitment came from; standing answers how it held up; a claim moves toward fact only by evidence or a gate; a capability whose basis changes is contested and re-reviewed, never silently updated in place.

The engine has no opinion about Google. It has invariants — and it checks whether Google's principles were enforced structure or unenforced prose.

§3

The instrument, in two layers

semantic containers over a contamination-free frame

The driver that runs this study is built the way Trellis builds anything that primes a model: invariants at the system layer, verdicts nowhere. Semantic tags carry the containers; a hypershot output frame carries the shape. The full prompt lives in docs/studies/turntrout-why-i-left-deepmind.metaprompt.md; its two load-bearing layers are below.

why free variablesThe frame primes the memo's shape and gives the analyst its primitives, but pins no content — so the study is not nudged toward a verdict, the way grounded authoring supplies a corpus and a template but never the citations. Primacy is the mechanism: put the shape first, and the undesired pattern (a pre-baked conclusion) never becomes a live option.

§4

The voice, codified

what “OpenCnid style” is, and the hypershot that holds it

The house register is legible once you name it: aphoristic doctrine lines balanced on a semicolon; layered em-dash sentences that stack precise qualifications; a load-bearing lexicon (substrate, standing, custody, contested, cover, global section, defeater, residual, incentive gradient, tooling shape); mechanism over disposition; and honesty markers that name a failure and keep its trail. Authority is stated as law — an invariant enforced at a single boundary, never diffused through convention. To reproduce it without copying any one sentence, prime the shape:
