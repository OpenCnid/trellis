## Why it is versioned here (the lesson that produced this directory)

Session 71 authored four judge rubrics and registered them as a
standing roster without ever reading `JUDGE_COMPOSITION_GAME.md` and
without invoking this skill. Both were available; neither was cited by
the session's handoff, and the handoff's starting points were treated
as the boundary of what was relevant. The skill living outside the
repository is part of why it was not inventoried as part of the
collection a session consults.

The resulting artifact asserted a default cast of four standing judges
— the precise thing the record's rule 4 forbids by closing
belief-facing taxonomies per composition. It was rolled back the same
session.

## What is invariant, and what is not

The distinction the session got wrong, stated plainly:

- **Four roles are invariant** — grounding, coherence, corroboration,
  audit — because each buys a blindness the others lack. The audit
  seat's failure taxonomy is invariant too: how judges fail does not
  depend on what they judge.
- **The judges filling those roles are not.** Registry selections and
  aspects, orientations, evidence channels, belief-facing taxonomies
  (defined fresh, closed before judging), and judge names all compose
  per context. The driving question sets registry access.

**There is no default cast.** A judge is a special case composed from
the semantic space of the facts and beliefs in the REPL, by
meta-prompting from primitives. The game's live run judged a
methodology claim with judges composed for it — there were no science
judges standing by, and no comedy judges for the hand before it. The
same four-role skeleton produced water-chemistry, comedy, and
methodology panels by swapping selections, orientations, and
taxonomies.

**Dated note — July 19, 2026 (collaborator direction, owner-relayed;
recorded here as direction, NOT as ratified record text).** Judges are
composed by an orchestrator running composition skills in the harness,
one agent per judge, built from the REPL's fact and belief space prior
to evaluation. This extends to the audit seat: its *name and angle*
compose per context and are load-bearing, while its failure taxonomy
stays largely fixed — what it does depends on what it is judging the
judges judge. The parameter space is vast; every judge is a special
case. Consequently the composition meta-prompts must be built and
tested, and until they are tested the system is trusted or simulated,
never assumed. Amending the record itself is an owner act by dated
entry; this note records the direction that prompts it.
