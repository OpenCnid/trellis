# Kept duplications — human rulings the ceremony honors

Repetition that a human has ruled **ceremonious** lives here. The dedupe
ceremony reads this file before it decides anything and leaves these claims
whole.

The list grows in one direction. Every ruling added makes the ceremony more
conservative than it was, which is the safe direction for a loop that runs
unattended: a claim, once protected, stays protected until a human says
otherwise.

**Adding an entry.** Name the claim, name every location, state which anchor
makes it ceremonious, and sign it with the date and who ruled. An entry without
an anchor is a claim waiting to be rehomed.

---

### §6 (Last look before you act) restates six rules as one-line pointers

**Locations:** `AGENTS.md` §6, and rules 1, 7, 16, 18, 19, 21 in §4.

**Anchor:** the section's own header — *"Pointers, not restatements — each
rule's text in §4 governs."* The label is explicit and the seams carry: §6 leads
in by naming itself a last look, and each line closes by citing the rule it
points at.

**Why it stays:** a pre-action checklist that forces a jump per item is a
checklist nobody runs. The copy fires at a moment §4 cannot reach the reader —
immediately before acting, rather than during study.

*Ruled 2026-07-23, owner and collaborator.*

### §1.5 (Authority ordering) and rule 18 cite each other

**Locations:** `AGENTS.md` §1.5 closing line, `AGENTS.md` §4 rule 18 closing
line.

**Anchor:** each is one clause, and each names the other as the thing it depends
on — §1.5 says rule 18 governs how you consume the chain, rule 18 says §1.5
carries the chain. Mutual cross-reference, both seams carrying.

**Why it stays:** a reader arriving from either direction needs the other, and
neither restates the other's content.

### Rule 20 keeps its entailment argument rather than pointing at rule 8

**Locations:** `AGENTS.md` §4 rule 8 (tooling shape closes failure classes;
prompt text reinforces), rule 20 (the same doctrine applied to testing).

**Anchor:** rule 20 names the relationship in its own text — *"rule 8, applied
to testing"* — and then carries the argument rather than the pointer.

**Why it stays:** the argument has to fire at the moment of temptation, when a
familiar comparison is easier to reach for than naming a target. A bare pointer
would not fire there. Same doctrine, different register: rule 8 states what
closes a failure class, rule 20 states what to do when about to measure one.
